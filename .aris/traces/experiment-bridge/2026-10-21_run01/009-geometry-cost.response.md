**Blocking finding identified and resolved during review.** The original wrapper created a new `GaussianModel` and immediately called `restore`, although only a different model had been initialized by `Scene`. The installed constructor does not define `_exposure`, while `restore` calls `training_setup`, which unconditionally constructs `Adam([self._exposure])`. That would fail before the first render. The revised wrapper, lines 59–65, supplies an independent parameter clone from the initialized Scene model, copies its exposure mapping, sets `pretrained_exposures=None`, and then restores the frozen model. This is a correct minimal fix in the wrapper. Exposure use remains disabled in every render, and no optimizer update occurs. I re-read the revised file and its syntax check passed. No official source change is necessary.
