# Native evaluation assumptions, fixed before any evaluation launch

- The only modes are `identity-only` and `complete`. Each requires a fresh output directory. Importing the script and asking for help do not initialize CUDA. This file does not authorize launch; the root task owns the external launcher.
- Both modes evaluate the same 32 sorted native official test cameras and the already inspected training camera `000016.jpg`. Training 016 is reported separately and never enters the 32-view mean, tail, or worst statistics. The test set is previously used exploratory evaluation.
- The native renderer uses SH degree 3, black background, no antialiasing, no trained exposure, native ordering/alpha and final RGB clamp. All model tensors are frozen; no optimizer, backward, training setup or parameter update is called. Model hashes and tensor identities are compared before/after.
- Identity uses the pinned author's actual `read_ply`, followed by the exact `store_ply` raw transformations: author `logit(op)` (clip to 1e-6 and 1-1e-6), `log(max(scale,1e-12))`, normalized reader quaternion, and unchanged position/SH mapping. No identity PLY is written. Conversion differences, including legal opacity clipping and quaternion normalization, are descriptive results, not zero-difference gates.
- Complete mode requires the successful CPU runner record (SHA-256 `5e9f4a1b120a50a708865bb4a242b0c9277eca08f0f2fc4ff86ce85570a27f27`) and the exact validated independent output PLY (SHA-256 `383dd0268752f341612231c4206c00bc7f5c737e374ae38c75f1f22f1aeeb7b8`) with 1,025,385 points and 62 float32 properties. It loads the standard raw PLY with native `GaussianModel.load_ply`. The all219 arm is the exact saved indices with SHA-256 `f6c9f4da05e064db5c94134c8ee241eb2188fb83a5c3f860655d2eaf279f992f`. Complete mode references and verifies prior successful identity outputs, and does not rerender the identity arm.
- Teacher/all219 replay is checked against the saved pruned-opacity-transfer 32-view full-float raw RGB, clamped exactly once to the native display range. Historical raw files are verified against their saved manifest. GT arrays must be identical. Maximum RGB difference must be at most 2e-5; all219 old GT-MSE and teacher-fidelity MSE differences at most 1e-9. Coverage-controls CSV PSNR and SSIM differences must be at most 2e-4 and 2e-5, respectively, including training 016. Failed source or replay identity stops the evaluation and preserves the failure. These are tool checks, not quality acceptance criteria.
- Save final native float32 RGB and GT for every evaluated image, with file hashes. Metrics use native torch float32 MSE/PSNR and native `utils.loss_utils.ssim`. Each arm reports its own largest-GT-MSE tail four and worst view. Mean PSNR and PSNR computed from mean MSE are distinct quantities. Teacher-fidelity errors and their own tail four are also retained.
- Complete-mode panels are fixed at training `000016.jpg` and test `000017.jpg`, `000105.jpg`, columns GT / teacher / all219 / NanoGS. Save the full image and the same top-band crop x=0,y=0,w=979,h=220 for all columns. Only final [0,1] RGB to 8-bit rounding is used; no tone adjustment or result-based crop selection.
- Record native Python/CUDA source, loaded extension binary, author source, camera transforms, used photograph source files, sparse calibration files, original PLY, subset indices and CPU output identities before/after. Existing native sparse/0/points3D.ply is required to prevent automatic conversion writes. Do not modify the teacher or source packages.
- Internal wall limit is 300 seconds for identity and complete evaluation combined, with a watchdog; complete mode deducts the saved identity stage wall from that limit. Root must also enforce the external limit. CUDA allocation limit is 10 GiB with both the allocator limit and sampled peak checks. Timing is total stage wall only and cannot support a rendering FPS claim. No training, simplification, parameter search, or new effects are performed by this script.
