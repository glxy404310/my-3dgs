No installation, source patch, training setup, optimizer construction, parameter update, GT loss, quality evaluation, or FPS measurement is permitted.

The stdlib supervisor launches two fresh processes with independent PYTHONPATH and module provenance checks. A uses the unchanged native helper/Scene to export canonical records for all 219 train cameras and all six raw parameter hashes, with zero render/backward. Existing points3D.ply is required to avoid loader conversion writes. Teacher/model/data are read-only inputs.

All six raw Gaussian fields remain `requires_grad=False`, `.grad is None`, and value hashes unchanged. Before scoring, exactly one extra fixed-Fisher forward on train `000016` is compared over the whole image to the SHA-pinned existing native teacher NPZ. Use the complete original math, unchunked on GPU: `torch.linalg.svdvals(fishers)` for `[N,6,6]`, then `torch.log(sv).sum(dim=1)`. No epsilon, ridge, finite-value replacement, CPU fallback, reduced SH, retry, changed pool resolution, or selected views. Subset all six raw fields by that exact mask, call original PUP `save_ply` into this new score directory, and require exact equality of every one of the teacher's 62 raw PLY properties for surviving rows. The unchanged teacher hash protects the source. Natural ties and -inf remain untouched. The upper point budget is 1,025,385: any real count in 1..1,025,385 may proceed to later native evaluation. Exact 50% is not a success gate; if the natural rule keeps fewer points, prohibit claims of equal point count and report the actual count.
